Renting a home in Dubai: a step-by-step guide

From setting a budget to Ejari and moving in, plus the rules on rent increases that every tenant should know.

By Plotmark Real Estate25 November 20265 min read

Key takeaways

  • Every Dubai tenancy contract must be registered through Ejari, the official system of the Real Estate Regulatory Agency.
  • Rent increases at renewal are limited by Decree No. 43 of 2013 and depend on how your rent compares with the official rental index.
  • Your landlord must give written notice of any change, including a rent increase, at least 90 days before the contract expires.

1. Set your budget

Dubai rents are quoted per year. Payment is usually made by post-dated cheques, and the number of cheques is often negotiable. Alongside the rent, budget for a refundable security deposit, the agency fee, Ejari registration and your DEWA (utilities) deposit.

2. Shortlist and view

Decide on the community, commute, size and whether you want furnished or unfurnished. View in person or by video, and check the building's facilities, parking and any service issues.

3. Agree the terms

Make an offer covering the rent, number of cheques, start date and anything the landlord will fix before you move in. Once agreed, the terms go into the standard tenancy contract.

4. Sign and register with Ejari

Dubai law requires tenancy contracts to be registered through Ejari. Registration gives the contract official status and is needed to connect utilities and for other government services. Your agent usually handles it.

5. Connect utilities and move in

Activate DEWA (electricity and water) using your Ejari, pay the deposit, and arrange gas, internet and cooling if they are separate. Record the condition of the home with photos on the day you move in.

Rent increases and renewals

Under Decree No. 43 of 2013, the maximum rent increase at renewal depends on how far your current rent is below the average for similar properties in the official rental index; no increase is allowed if your rent is within 10% of the average. Any change to the terms, including the rent, must be notified in writing at least 90 days before the contract expires. Disputes go to the Rental Disputes Centre.
